graphedwright

a graph drawn and edited in your browser, from Go compiled to WebAssembly

Click a shape or an edge to select it, drag a shape to move it, drag from a port or a shape's edge to connect, drag a shape from the palette to add one, click a line kind to draw that kind (again to let the shapes choose); drag the square on a selected shape's corner to resize it; double-click, Enter, F2 or just type to edit a label; scroll to pan, Ctrl+scroll, the + − Fit buttons or Ctrl+= Ctrl+- Ctrl+0 to zoom, Alt+drag or the middle button to pan; Delete, arrows, Ctrl+Z.

Properties

Select one shape or edge to see its properties.
